17) CAN COMPANY NOT REACH RS. 1500 CRORES REVENUE EARLIER THAN 
2030-31 ? 
 
A company like ours and the business that we are in have certain advantages like high entry 
barriers due to high capital requirement, long time required in registration and approvals 
with all users, highly skilled and experienced manpower requirement and higher margins. 
Businesses like these cannot be easily scaled up very fast and any rapid growth will result 
into capability mismatch leading to margin deterioration. The company is aiming for 
margin improvements and does not plan as of now to go on a path of high growth by 
sacrificing margins.  
 
To exceed Rs. 1500 crores revenue not only we have to build up our infrastructure by 
investing about Rs. 200 crores in our various facilities between April 2025 & March 2030 
but also built up a strong team to handle Rs. 1500 crores business. We are a custom 
engineered products company and not a mass production company and for such businesses, 
building team takes time. Hence even if investment of Rs. 200 crores can be done in one 
go it will not be advisable to do so as pace of building infrastructure and pace of building 
a team is different. Ideally we will like to invest gradually in both ie in infrastructure as 
well as in team building so as to be ready to cater Rs. 1500 crores business without 
stretching the entire operations. This will also allow us enough time to grow the market and 
grow the product range so as to cater to increased manufacturing capability.  
 
We are targeting an annual growth of between 12-15% in next 5 years and on the basis of 
this we expect to cross Rs. 1500 crores turnover by 2030-31.
